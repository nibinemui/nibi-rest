04 / Ready, trainer?

Getting into
the playtest.

The playtest runs on Windows. You'll need your own copy of Pokémon Black or White.

01

Prepare your game

Have your own US or Europe Pokémon Black or Pokémon White Nintendo DS ROM dump ready. The game itself is not included.

02

Launch the client

Download and open the Castelia Online client on a Windows 10 or Windows 11 64-bit PC. Select your game and set up your trainer.

03

Connect to Unova

Connect to the playtest server and step into the world. Your online save is managed by the server while connected.

Playtest note: This is an early community build. Online play uses a server-authoritative save, and features may change as development continues. Cheats and save states are blocked while online. Client downloads and detailed setup instructions will be linked here when the public release page is ready.
