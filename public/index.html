Before you ask

Questions, answered.

Something not covered here? Ask in the Discord.

What is Unova Online?

A fan-made multiplayer layer for Pokémon Black and White. The games run as they always have, in a client built on the melonDS emulator, and our server adds the other players: name tags, chat, trading, link battles, missions and more, all inside the game.

Do I need my own copy of the game?

Yes. Unova Online needs your own Pokémon Black or Pokémon White (US). The game is not included, and we can't share it.

Can Black and White players play together?

Yes. Both games share one world. You'll see players from either version, and you can trade and battle with them.

What happens to my save?

Every new account starts a brand-new adventure. Each time you save in the game, a copy goes to the server, so your progress is kept safe and follows your account.

Can I play offline, or use cheats and save states?

No. The game only runs while connected, and cheats and save states are turned off, so trades, battles and rankings stay fair for everyone.

How many people can play at once?

Unova is split into copies of up to 20 trainers each, so towns stay lively without getting crowded. Switch copies from the C-Gear, or tap Join on a friend's profile to land in theirs.

What are Castelia Coins?

The online currency. You earn them by playing, battling, trading and finishing missions, and spend them on trainer looks and on the Items board.

What do I need to run it?

For the playtest: a 64-bit Windows 10 or 11 PC and an internet connection.

When can I play?

The first playtest build is ready, and the download goes up on this page when the playtest opens. Join the Discord to hear the moment it does.
